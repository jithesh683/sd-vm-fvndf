PFMEA Effects & Severity Helper

Import your existing PFMEA Excel template. The tool fills the output columns in the same worksheet and keeps the existing rows, columns, and other sheets wherever supported. Export a completed copy in the original workbook layout.
Upload your existing Excel template. Input columns are Product Characteristic, Process Characteristic, Criteria, Symbol. Output fields are filled in matching existing columns; missing output columns are added at the end. Original workbook data and other sheets are retained.
Rules used:
  • Suggestions are based on the characteristic text and criteria/symbol provided.
  • If the characteristic is not recognized by the built-in rules, the effect and all ratings are N/A rather than guessed.
  • Export preserves the uploaded worksheet layout and populates matching output columns; check formatting after export if your template uses complex merged cells or macros.
  • Severity, Revised Detection, and Revised Occurrence are preliminary suggestions (1–10) for review. Detection and occurrence values are not verified from your actual controls or defect history. Confirm severity, detection, and occurrence against your company/customer-approved PFMEA rating tables, actual controls, and historical defect data.
